What it is

Paragonday replaces the clock question “what time is it?” with the one your body actually asks: how much daylight is left? It sits quietly in your menu bar and shows Horizon Time — time measured from sunrise and sunset where you are.

  • −2:41 tilset during the day — 2 h 41 m until sunset
  • −7:12 tilrise at night — 7 h 12 m until the next sunrise
  • Dual display: time since sunrise and until sunset at once
  • Optional UTC row for coordinating across horizons
  • Automatic location, or type in any latitude/longitude
  • Works offline with a built-in astronomical fallback (±1 minute)

Install

  1. Download Paragonday.app.zip and unzip it
  2. Drag Paragonday.app into your Applications folder
  3. Right-click the app → Open → Open the first time you launch it
  4. Allow location access (used only to compute sunrise/sunset), or choose Use manual location… from the menu

The right-click step is needed because the app isn’t notarized with Apple yet — macOS shows a warning on a plain double-click. The app is open source, so you can also build it yourself.
